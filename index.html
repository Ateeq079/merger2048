Welcome to 2048 Merge, a tile-sliding puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (if any), how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By playing 2048 Merge, you agree to the practices described below.

1 Information We Collect

2048 Merge does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The game is designed to be played entirely offline without requiring an account or login.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts access
  • Any form of biometric data

2 Local Data Storage

To save your game progress, high scores, and settings, 2048 Merge stores data locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

3 Third-Party Services

2048 Merge does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or social media SDKs that collect user data.

If future versions introduce optional third-party services (such as leaderboards), this policy will be updated before those features go live and you will be notified.

4 App Permissions

The game may request the following minimal Android permissions solely to function correctly:

  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback on tile merges (optional)
  • INTERNET — only if required by the underlying Godot engine; no user data is transmitted

No sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, location, etc.) are requested or used.

5 Children's Privacy

2048 Merge is suitable for all ages and designed to be safe for children. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no special concerns regarding children's data under COPPA or similar regulations.

If you believe a child has inadvertently submitted personal information through some other means, please contact us immediately using the email below.

6 Data Security

Because all game data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data depends on your device's own security settings (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

We do not have access to any data on your device and therefore cannot retrieve, modify, or delete it on your behalf.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the Effective Date at the top of this page
  • Publish the revised policy at the same URL
  • If changes are significant, notify users through an in-app notice on the next update

Continued use of 2048 Merge after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
